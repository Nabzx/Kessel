# Retained robustness-failure ranking plan

24 November 2026. Diagnostic continuation of ADR-0062, after ADRs 0063/0064.
This authorises no controller, simulator, observation-contract or runtime change.

## Scope and cost

Read the already consumed 416-case stress catalogue once, including all three
frozen neural sources and the separate conventional reference. Preserve nominal,
left-receiver and farther-receiver cases as context; rank the other ten families.
The receiver-frame adapter has only nominal/farther qualification. These old
traces do not establish its performance on any other family.

Use Python standard-library analysis only, one serial process, no GPU, policy
queries, teacher queries, environment steps or optimiser updates. Check a
60-second active-analysis cap between cases. Reject decompressed traces above
32 MiB and output above 2 MiB. Write repo-local output once, never overwrite.
Hash the input report, archive, exact archived controller/observation sources,
this plan and the analysis source. Verify every trace digest and case identity.
Record measured duration and all 416 case metrics. A timeout or coverage error
must fail rather than silently omit cases. Do not expand the budget after a
failure. No browser, server, remote operation or new qualification run.

## Measurements

Recompute per-source successes, collisions and termination counts. Pair each
neural outcome with the reference on the same family/base seed; report reference
success/neural failure separately from shared failures. Do not infer physical
infeasibility from a reference failure.

For every trace retain terminal task phase, phase occupancy, first phase-entry
times, peak payload, cumulative material transfers and initial available mass.
Retain true final state and the last input/action separately. Distinguish failed
pickup, loaded carry, partial delivery and collisions without assuming a unique
cause from a terminal label. Check material conservation and frame continuity.

Measure range-valid fractions, freshness and age, commands during complete
invalidity, physical displacement, observed contact cues and physical pickup
before the conventional memory leaves approach. Physical pickup comes from
the retained transfer ledger; a range-height cue is not physical contact truth.
Report the final 50-decision carry speed/drive and receiver-mouth error to help
distinguish loaded arrival stalls from earlier failures. No counterfactual
queries or new dynamics are needed.

## Ranking and interpretation

Prioritise observed collision families first. Within those, explicit missing-range
signals offer the most direct bounded next target; blocked-corridor collisions
need an observation-contract audit, then shifted starts and low piles. Next
prioritise impaired range, where the reference completes all eight settings.
Then shared non-collision failures, ordered by neural delivery shortfall and
with pile/traction mechanisms kept separate. This is an engineering work order,
not a statistical risk score or prevalence estimate.

Freeze that order as: range_dropout, blocked, start_offset, low, range_impaired,
steep, ridge, low_grip, high_resistance, rubble. Ridge precedes low grip because
its retained neural shortfall is 10 rather than 9 of 24. Do not pool reference
and neural outcomes to improve a gate. Report all three neural seeds separately.

The proposed next implementation must state the available input, observed
failure, missing evidence, success/stop distinction and bounded follow-up needed.
No observed command, lack of collision or early termination alone proves a safe
stop. Missing-range stopping and blocked-path handling need their own future
protocols and fresh qualification. This ranking completes diagnosis only.
