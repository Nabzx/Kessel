## Result

The original nominal proficiency result remains valid for its declared domain.
The new frozen policies **do not meet a broad robustness gate**. Only nominal
and left-shifted-receiver families pass the predeclared threshold for every
training seed: at least 7/8 complete empty-start deliveries and zero collisions.
No actor is tuned or reselected against these results.

Eight fresh base seeds are transformed into thirteen named families, 104
synthetic settings. Every setting is run by all three frozen phase-memory
actors and the separate conventional reference: **416 policy/scenario cases**.
Shared base geometry across families and shared cases across policies are
intentional paired comparisons, not independent mines. Counts below are
complete deliveries, each out of eight.

Across this deliberately mixed stress catalogue the learned policies complete
131/312 cases, with 60 collisions; the conventional reference completes 57/104,
with 25 collisions. These are **catalogue counts**, not a mine-operating success
rate. Expected blocked failures are never counted as deliveries. A blocked
collision is a failure, not demonstrated hazard avoidance or a safe stop.
Eight cases per family are a small diagnostic sample, not a reliability estimate.

The full scorecard includes collisions, termination reasons, carried/delivered/
spilled mass, all-case duration, successful-case duration and per-source inference
latencies. No average hides failed runs or compares early collisions as fast jobs.
All actions are generated by the selected actor, with conventional phase memory
explicitly retained. The reference is a different controller, not a fallback.

## What the failures distinguish

Moving the receiver farther back gives the clearest policy-specific gap: the
reference completes 8/8 while all neural policies complete 0/8. Impaired range
also separates the reference from the learners. Investigate training coverage,
closed-loop action extrapolation and the phase/observation contract on new
training/validation cases before changing the actors.

Low/steep piles and low grip expose limits shared with the reference. Reference
failure does not prove physical infeasibility. It may reflect controller logic,
task budget or the simplified simulator. The offset start produces collisions
for both controller types. Missing range does not trigger a validated stopping
behaviour; none is claimed. Prioritise these categories before any richer visual
scene or transfer-to-machine assertion.

It accepts test bases 206200–206207 and separate calibration 108400–108407 with
no matches or unreadable archives. Scope is available retained local evidence,
not unknown external experiments. All derived families stay with their base split.

Python/PyTorch/NumPy environment. There are **zero new actor updates** and zero

The study executes **154,244 evaluation decisions** and a second simulator
replays the same **154,244 decisions**, with zero state error. Its active duration

evaluation decisions. All 416 trajectories and outcomes are retained in
[traces.zip](shift-evidence/traces.zip), including failures.

A separate action verifier recomputes every learned trace and the eight
calibration traces: **121,530 policy queries**, zero action/phase error, zero

zero state error. Those are verification costs on consumed cases, not additional

## Independent simulator probes

The [simulator audit](continuation-evidence/simulator.json) replays consumed scene
207600 with conventional control. All nine contact/discretisation cases conserve
material within 3.64e-12 kg and replay exactly. Configured substeps 1 and 5 both
use the kernel's minimum five physical steps per 0.1-second decision, so they
are not independent resolutions. Ten substeps use 10 ms increments.

The rubble case changes from a wall collision at 20 ms to a time limit with
1,378.76 kg delivered at 10 ms. Legacy and steep cases complete at both effective
resolutions, with small delivery differences. This is evidence of contact-
discretisation sensitivity, not calibrated granular physics. Do not silently
change the reference dynamics or merge results from the two resolutions.

Full-command probes reach the documented drive, articulation and bucket bounds;
the boom probe measures its rate over three seconds. Incremental reward probes
show no pickup-only credit, positive delivery credit and penalties for spillage,
time, effort and collision. A positive delivery reward does not prove that PPO
can discover the sequence. The new successful controller is supervised imitation,
not a reward-only learning result.

## Working demonstration and next experiment

The [live route](live-demo/README.md) executes fresh physics and neural actions
locally, with reset, run/pause, step, source/scenario selection and trace export.
It restricts interactive seeds to practice data, which may overlap training,
and retains failed outcomes. A recorded fallback never silently replaces it.
The main authored walkthrough remains separate.

The next learning experiment should vary receiver distance, initial pose,
pile shape and observation quality within training, retain all stages in replay,
and compare phase-conditioned and simpler baselines with matched data costs.
First diagnose shared reference failures and define missing-observation behaviour.
Predeclare a new split and per-family gate before any policy query. The consumed
stress cases can diagnose gaps but can no longer serve as untouched final tests.
Neither this study nor the interactive viewer qualifies real equipment.
