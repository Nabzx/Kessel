# ADR-0053: Guided task and engineering chapters

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Complete #349 under the maintainer's instruction to work through the remaining
walkthrough issues. #351 remains a visual judgement, not a barrier to building
the concrete experience the maintainer has authorised. Retain review evidence;
automated checks never constitute the maintainer's visual acceptance.

The learning entry is a single complete, explicitly conventional reference cycle.
Other reference conditions, measured policy checkpoints and curriculum seeds stay
available in the inspector, including their failures. A replay ending correctly
is different from a learned policy completing its task. No new success is claimed.
Use a constant simulated-time rate within a source and a finite terminal hold.
Frame the full articulated machine and working area against actual interface
rectangles, recomputing on resize. Keep source records unchanged.

The primary panel shows task phase, payload, delivery and simulated time. Charts,
source/seed selection, forecast horizons and provenance use progressive disclosure.
Optional perception, prediction, training/export, offline-operation and fleet-loop
chapters must separate recorded observations from proposed architecture. They do
not lengthen the required investor route. No live training, measured Jetson
latency, deployed fleet or successful learned full cycle is implied.

Keep the maintainer's current Orbitron hero and emerald interface accent. This
supersedes the amber-only and font-pair restrictions in ADRs 0051/0052 following
the merged home work. Operating failures remain readable through words and shape.

Use a prepared fade for both arrival transitions. A roof-off section appears only
after its assets are ready, with no flight through incompatible coordinate systems.
Original Blender geometry and the existing cleared PBR library supply close detail.
Public Rwanda geography supplies context, never a claim of a surveyed concession.
The proposed kit uses dimensioned mechanical construction and an assembled-first
breakdown. Headless Blender may rebuild the same reviewed source when the pinned
MCP endpoint is unavailable; no hosted asset service or runtime connector is added.

Release/publication tasks remain excluded. Build tickets receive pending-release
only after merge; final visual judgement remains with the maintainer.
