# ADR-0060: Recorded demo and model evidence

Status: Proposed

Refs #412–#414 and #429–#434.

## Recommendation

Keep learned world models experimental until a useful model-free policy and
an independently qualified forecast gate exist. The latest assistance study
has no accepted imagined transitions or learned complete cycles. Preserve the
earlier and latest negative outcomes and imposed-filter distinctions.

Improve distinct rare-event episode coverage before repeating weighting of
the same handful of failures. Compare observable, privileged, persistence,
kinematic and constrained methods on common cases. Report per-field units,
multistep drift, event false-safe/false-stop and validation-only calibration.
Count all data/fitting/evaluation costs before any assistance-efficiency claim.

Use hash-validated recordings for the first technical demo interface. They
can show real synthetic experiments without browser training or a new runtime.
Offer finite supported scenario cases; do not present recording selection as
an arbitrary physics rerun. Freeze forecast anchors and preserve terminal
outcomes. Commit clock, camera, source and metrics together when switching.

Live simulator/inference and browser-compatible inference are separate optional
spikes. Keep their measured identity and failure behaviour distinct from a
recorded fallback. Retained CPU ONNX numbers do not qualify browser or proposed
edge hardware performance. Prioritise fidelity and useful behaviour before
compressing an unsuccessful actor.

Keep the current concept presentation until a production integration decision
is accepted. A technical evidence route can explain verified failures now;
proficiency claims require a predeclared complete-task gate later. Require
executed checkpoint/controller identity, scenario/split identity, actual
trajectories, independent replay and matching static-export/source labels.
Invalidate results after relevant source/model/task changes.

## Evidence and implementation sequence

[World-model recommendation](../research/2026-11-13-world-model-recommendation.md),
[runtime/control report](../research/2026-11-13-demo-runtime-and-controls.md) and
[admission report](../research/2026-11-13-evidence-admission.md) compare options,
retained measurements, criteria and limitations. Existing verifiers protect
the old evidence; the new frontier audit extracts compact current summaries.

Implement the recorded catalogue and admission checks after the observation,
scenario and skill protocols are settled. Preserve all failed outcomes and
existing verification/release work items. No new model training, live runtime,
publication, human comprehension result or successful learned task is approved
or claimed by this research proposal.
