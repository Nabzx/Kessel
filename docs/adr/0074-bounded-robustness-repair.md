# ADR-0074: Bounded robustness repair

Status: Accepted for the same maintainer request. Research outcomes may fail;
no fresh qualification, runtime admission or field claim is pre-authorised.

## Decision and boundaries

Work through every original stress family using consumed practice cases only.
Separate phase/pickup feasibility, steering, observation degradation and physical
traction/resistance limits. No edits to frozen kernels, actors or evidence.

Add a separately named observation-only conventional task controller as a
feasibility comparison. It may correct reverse steering and retry an underfilled
tip through another collection pass. Keep the original 2,000 kg empty-start task,
600-decision limit and actual collisions. Resetting physics, spawning material,
continuing after termination or relabelling a partial delivery is forbidden.

Evaluate two declared fill-time variants (6 and 12 seconds). Select by total
collision-free successes across practice bases 19010/19011; break ties in favour
of 12 seconds. Rehearsal/selection is charged separately from later evaluation.
Three neural candidates initialise from the retained 7/19/41 actors and receive
only training-partition teacher labels from the selected comparison. Their
continuous commands remain neural; conventional phase memory and input smoothing
are declared. Preserve missing-cell masks. Compare filtered/unfiltered input on
validation only before frozen evaluation. Do not compose the qualified receiver
and stopping methods or imply their existing qualifications transfer.

Use all thirteen original families. Collect teacher episodes on 19010/19011;
candidate validation uses 19012 and evaluation uses 19013. These are distinct
practice episodes, not audited untouched tests. Train each actor for at most
1,000 minibatch updates (512 rows). No DAgger or additional tuning after evaluation.
Select input smoothing by validation complete deliveries then collisions, with
ties favouring unfiltered input. Retain all teacher failures and neural outcomes.

Cap all collection/rehearsal/validation/evaluation at 100,000 physical decisions,
teacher calls at 30,000, optimiser updates at 3,000, one CPU thread and 600 seconds.
Store source/weight/data hashes, all cases, commands, phase and material ledgers.
Replay all final neural applied actions with a separate simulator, at most
25,000 additional decisions within the same wall cap. No runtime is broadened.

## Completion

Report per-family/per-seed delivery, collisions, stop outcomes, underfilled
pickup and actual reference feasibility. A failed family stays failed even when
another metric improves. Retain the complete finite experiment and a reproducible
CI verifier. Further research requires a new protocol rather than silently
expanding budgets. No browser or service is required. Merge this stage separately.
