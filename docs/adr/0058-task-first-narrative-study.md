# ADR-0058: Task-first walkthrough narrative study

Status: Proposed

Refs #407, #415 and #416.

## Context

The maintainer requests exploration of the walkthrough narrative before the
remaining research topics. The primary opening spends three chapters on
context; the rail already supports direct navigation. The current authored
training success and retained failed experiments have distinct source identities.
They must not become one apparent successful learned trajectory.

The [narrative report](../research/2026-11-13-walkthrough-narrative.md) documents
alternatives, script, camera constraints, source transitions and a review rubric.
The isolated [interactive study](../phase-11/narrative-study/index.html)
compares mine-first and current-opening routes with the same explanatory body.

## Recommendation

Propose a mine-first primary story: task, observation, learned prediction,
policy learning and proposed retrofit. Retain Earth, environment selection and
surface as optional context. Use an editorial target of about one minute for
the core explanation, without treating that as a measured viewing result.

Use the same recorded reference episode through task/observation/prediction.
Explicitly seek to the relevant observation or forecast anchor. Mark the switch
to the learned-policy failure as a different recording. Mark the hardware
explanation as proposed architecture. Preserve authored illustration as its own
source if retained in a future production route.

Use a fixed whole-job camera fitted to visible source/receiver/machine geometry
over all recorded poses and midpoints. Make contact detail optional and disclose
its loss of context. Never alter a pose to keep it in frame. Place observed
samples in their actual frame and use only retained forecast/actual pairs.
Magnify small forecast differences in a separately labelled plot with physical
units, not an enlarged ghost object inside the mine.

Keep the primary explanation short. Put metrics and optional controls below
the source boundary, and detailed record identities behind disclosure. Verify
the target laptop layout, keyboard access, reduced motion, missing-data recovery
and source continuity. Conduct the documented comprehension review before
claiming that the route improves first-time understanding.

## Scope and conflicts

This research adds an isolated prototype, screenshots and recommendations.
It does not supersede ADR-0054/0055 or change the accepted production default.
Adopting the proposed primary entry requires an accepted superseding decision
and a scoped implementation. No new cinematic environment, browser training,
hardware validation, publication or successful learned delivery is implied.

The prototype is a communication study using selected declared records, not
a product MVP, benchmark aggregate or evidence that the chosen policy works.
Research output and automated checks do not replace human visual judgement
on #351/#385 or the unperformed comprehension review.
