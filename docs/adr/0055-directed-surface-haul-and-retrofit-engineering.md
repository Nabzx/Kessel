# ADR-0055: Directed surface haul and retrofit engineering

Status: Accepted

The maintainer requests correction of the pipeline cards and surface truck,
and authorises a substantial redesign of the proposed retrofit presentation.
This supersedes ADR-0054's instruction to retain the previous kit presentation.
Its source distinctions, resumable chapters and no-release boundary remain.

Use four aligned pipeline stages with consistent icon, label, description and
formula rows. Avoid inheriting inconsistent minimum heights and decorative
borders from the previous product layout.

Replace the circular surface loop with one finite haul: wait, accelerate,
follow a smooth ninety-degree yard bend, brake and hold. Use one retained
metre-scale trace for the Blender film and real-time fallback. Explicitly set
Blender rigid-pivot rotation modes; verify evaluated world transforms against
travel direction, tyre rotation, steering and ground contact. Replay is an
explicit action and returning resumes the same scene position.

Make the retrofit a dedicated engineering presentation. Provide a detailed
assembly, exploded inspection, component selection, spatial callouts and a
local sensing/policy/vehicle-interface signal view. Use the original hardware
geometry and researched engineering references, with short precise component
descriptions and proposed interface/packaging details. Do not invent measured
Jetson performance, selected production hardware, environmental certification
or a validated safety system. Retained CPU evidence remains identified as CPU
evidence. Any animated signal stream is illustrative architecture.

Preserve the accepted globe, environment selector, mine and training chapters.
Retain keyboard controls, reduced motion, state on navigation and native M1
Chrome verification. No public release or deployment is part of this change.
