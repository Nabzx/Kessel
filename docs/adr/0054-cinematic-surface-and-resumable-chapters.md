# ADR-0054: Cinematic surface and resumable chapters

Status: Accepted

## Context

The maintainer finds the surface too game-like and the recorded experiments
confusing in the investor journey. He approves a Blender-rendered animated
surface trial, a single authored training demonstration, three complete jobs
including obstacle avoidance, a bottom chapter rail and resuming chapter state.
The technical product chapters and proposed kit are retained.

## Decision

Use an original locally rendered surface film with a still fallback. Supply
properly cleared PBR materials, plausible machinery/human scale, contact shadows
and tyre-relative dust. A local video is scene content, not observed mine footage.
It shares the presentation's pause/resume and reduced-motion lifecycle. Keep the
real-time surface available as a load-failure fallback during the trial.

Replace the default recorded-experiment entry in ADR-0053 with one authored,
explicitly illustrative training sequence. Show a missed scoop and partial pickup,
then three complete scoop/haul/tip jobs. One job detects and routes around a solid
obstacle. Preserve finite speeds, ground contact, bucket clearance, wheel travel,
route/obstacle separation and a material ledger. A coherent kinematic demonstration
is not a validated granular-physics engine or a successful learned policy. Keep
recorded experiments and their failed outcomes as research evidence, outside the
main demonstration controls. Remove unexplained prediction boxes.

Use a persistent bottom rail for Earth, environments, surface, mine, training,
Inside Kessel and retrofit. Full-screen chapter hand-offs replace ordinary scrolling.
Preserve each chapter's state, including film time, training progress, selected
technical chapter and kit assembly. Prepare destinations before reveal; the latest
navigation request wins. Keep keyboard access and reduced motion.

The maintainer explicitly authorises closing already implemented tickets without
waiting for a tagged release. This supersedes the closure rule in AGENTS.md and
ADR-0053. Close merged implementation tickets; leave unfinished/review/release work
open. This is tracker maintenance, not authorisation to publish or tag a release.

Round Enter the mine with an emerald hover/focus state. Enlarge Discover Kessel.
Remove the second world-model entry from the product section. Preserve the existing
hero, trained mine, technical chapters and working retrofit unless integration
requires a small change.
