What you are seeing

A real location.
An illustrative mission.

Kessel is developing physical AI for extreme environments. This experience illustrates the approach. The training chapter is an authored kinematic demonstration. Technical chapters use identified experiment records and explain the proposed architecture. The site performs no live training or machine control.
