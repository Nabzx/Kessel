CONCEPT DEMO / SCRIPTED SCENARIO

WHAT YOU ARE SEEING

Real location. Illustrative mission.

Kessel is developing physical AI for extreme environments. This concept demo uses a scripted scenario to explain the approach. It does not run a trained policy or 100,000 live simulations.

Location
Rutongo Mines, Rwanda. Public coordinates and satellite terrain; the portal, workings and fleet are illustrative. Trinity Metals ↗
Earth and terrain
NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble, historical USGS MRDS mineral occurrences, modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2026 and Copernicus WorldDEM-30. Full asset credits ↗
Vehicles and results
Original Kessel vehicle geometry, with a compact 15 t underground-truck reference envelope. Routes, cycle times, prediction errors and performance improvements are authored scenario inputs. Equipment reference ↗
Mine assets
Rock Wall 09, Brown Mud Dry and Aerial Rocks 02 by Poly Haven, CC0. Excavator 3d Rigging by mohamed.salaah880, CC BY 4.0; scaled and animated for the loading scene. Excavator attribution ↗
Surface satellite view
Google Maps supplies the embedded satellite map and its provider credits. The local 3D site uses the separate Copernicus terrain source. No Google imagery is stored in this repository.
The product
Train inside a learned world model. Compress the policy onto a rugged retrofit computer. Operate offline. Improve the world model from deployment data when a connection becomes available.
