What you are seeing

      
A real location.
An illustrative mission.

      

        Kessel is developing physical AI for extreme environments. This
        experience illustrates the approach. The optional recorded experiment
        replays identified checkpoints and raw world-model forecasts from a
        synthetic simulator. It performs no live training or machine control.

Learning and performance

        

          Mine operating figures and savings are illustrative. Recorded mode
          uses actual synthetic policy decisions and raw model forecasts; its
          bounded runs completed no full learned delivery cycle. Concept replay
          remains authored. The displayed kit and onboard deployment are proposed.
          World-model research;
          policy distillation.
