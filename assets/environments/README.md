# Environment reference photographs

These five locally served WebP previews are reference environments, not
Kessel deployments or surveys. In particular, the underground photograph
shows the NIOSH research coal mine in Pennsylvania, not Rutongo, Rwanda.
The visible figure captions identify reference imagery and the provider.
The Sources dialog retains full individual credits and original pages.

- Glacier: NASA/USGS, Jakobshavn Isbrae, Greenland, Landsat 8 (2022), PIA26117.
- Desert: Earth Science and Remote Sensing Unit, NASA Johnson Space Center,
  Grand Erg Oriental, Algeria, ISS046-E-48626.
- Space: NASA/Bill Anders, Apollo 8 Earthrise, 24 December 1968.
- Deep sea: NOAA Ocean Exploration, 2016 Deepwater Exploration of the Marianas.
- Underground: CDC/NIOSH; Chad Hutson, 2013; PHIL 19314. CDC is the free source.

The individual source records identify government photography/data and its
credit. NASA media guidance and NOAA/CDC notices apply. No endorsement is
implied. See `manifest.json` for the exact source URLs, notices, original and
derivative hashes and transformations. No third-party copyrighted image is
silently cleared merely because it appears on a government website.

Prepared with `scripts/prepare-environment-previews.py` and isolated Pillow
13.0.0: EXIF orientation, rectangular crop, Lanczos resize to 768 × 432 and
WebP quality 84. No synthesis or content retouching. Originals stay outside
the published site. All five derivatives total 277,482 bytes.
